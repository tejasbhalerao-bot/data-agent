Q2 Roadmap

The Logistics Growth Loop

"Logistics isn't a cost center that supports growth — it's a growth lever in its own right."

The Mechanism

Every point of promise accuracy rewards twice:

  1. A lower ETA at checkout wins the order
  2. A kept promise at the doorstep wins the customer back

Repeat orders and improved conversion raise order density per lane, sharpening our ETA model, earning us the right to tighten the promise further.

Growth this quarter compounds our ability to grow next quarter.

Governance

The loop has one condition: promises get shorter only as fast as accuracy allows. Aggressive ETAs that we can't consistently keep don't win customers — they generate RTOs, erode trust, and inflate cost.

On-time delivery is non-negotiable. It is not a metric we optimise when convenient — it is the flywheel that makes everything else work.
