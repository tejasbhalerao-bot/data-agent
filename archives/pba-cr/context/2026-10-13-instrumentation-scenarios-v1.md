# PBA instrumentation scenarios and question guide

Captured from the engineering artifact "PBA Instrumentation Records" on 2026-10-13. All records go to `logistics_instrumentation`; they join on `request_id` and `reference_id` (the order id). Shipment actuals are not in these records; they come from shipment tracking, joined on the order id.

| Record type | Status | One record per | Scenarios in artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| PBA_ALLOCATION_INSTRUMENTATION | Live (MR1); records pinned by the SLT suite | PBA call (serviceability / LMS allocator): cache rows read, per-partner lookup, cache age, cutoff window, refreshes, answer | 120 |
| ETA_INSTRUMENTATION | Proposed (MR2) | GetPromiseETA call (soft allocation): rollouts, every candidate source, decision, promise for both PBA and internal | 12 |
| HARD_ALLOCATION_INSTRUMENTATION | Proposed (MR2) | TPS cpRecommendation call (hard allocation): ClickPost, internal pick, LMS call, edit-order, buffers, partner served | 9 |

Field definitions: `2026-10-13-instrumentation-field-dictionary-v1.csv` (one row per field path, per record type, with values, null-when, renamed-from and live/proposed status).
Full ETA and HARD scenario records: `2026-10-13-instrumentation-scenario-records-eta-hard-v1.json`. PBA_ALLOCATION full records stay in the artifact (not copied).

**Caution:** the artifact's ETA/HARD field layout (`evaluation.rollout`, `evaluation.delivery_partner`, `evaluation.promises`, `evaluation.times`) differs from the two DRAFT JSONs engineering shared on 2026-10-13 (`evaluation.facts`, `evaluation.configurations`, `evaluation.results`, `evaluation.candidates[]`). See the source digest, open question Q1.
